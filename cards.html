How to play
  • A coin toss decides who goes first. The first player's cards are Red, the other player's are Blue.
  • Take turns placing a card from your hand on any empty square.
  • When you place a card, any touching opponent card whose facing number is lower than your card's number on that side flips to your color. One card can flip several at once.
  • Win, and you may add a copy of one of your opponent's cards that ended in your color to your collection. Cards you don't have yet are marked NEW.
